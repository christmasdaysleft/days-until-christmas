For grown-ups

Parents’ questions

How do I explain the countdown to young children?

Counting sleeps is easier for young children than counting days. Each morning, check the number together – when it says one sleep, Christmas is tomorrow.

Is the letter to Santa sent anywhere?

No. The letter is created and printed in your own browser and nothing is sent or saved online. Many families post the printed letter, pop it in the fireplace or leave it out on Christmas Eve.

How does the elf name generator work?

Your elf name is made from the first letter of your name and the month of your birthday, so it is always the same – you can share it and compare with friends and family.

When should we write a letter to Santa?

Many families write their letter at the start of December so there is plenty of time before Christmas Eve. If you want a reply through a national post office programme, check their deadline, which is often in early December.

What is Santa called in other countries?

He is Père Noël in France, Babbo Natale in Italy and Sinterklaas in the Netherlands. In parts of Germany and Austria the Christkind brings the presents, and in Russia it is Ded Moroz.

Is this page safe for kids?

Yes. There are no ads, no sign-ups and no chat features, and everything children type stays on the device.
